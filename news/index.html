Nocito 1.2.0 — what’s new

RELEASE_DATE

New home screen (Croatian, Price Intelligence)

  • One big card shows right away where your basket is cheaper and how much you save, with a bar per store.
  • Below it are four tiles: your items on sale, barcode scan, voice search and my items.
  • Adding an expense is behind one “+” button: snap a receipt, dictate, import or type it in.
  • The row with your stores and the “+” stays at the bottom of the screen, so nothing overlaps on smaller phones.

Shopping list

  • Make a list that looks like a rolled piece of paper and just tick items off in the store.
  • Add items by typing, from your own items, by voice or by scanning a barcode.
  • Each list is remembered by its date. You can open an old list, copy it into a new one or delete it.
  • Send the list as a message (for example on WhatsApp) and import a list when someone sends you theirs.
  • One tap compares the prices of the items on your list across your stores.

Prices and comparisons

  • “Provjereno” (Checked): everything you scanned or spoke, with prices per store and a bell to follow a deal.
  • Best basket: a comparison of the whole basket by store; the first one is the cheapest.
  • “Poveži s cjenicima” (Link to price lists): cards you swipe left or right. At the end of a round it says how many items are still waiting and you can carry on.
  • Price changes and What you could have saved: two new reports. If you do not have enough receipts with items, the app offers to pull them out of old receipts and tells you how many scans that uses.
  • The Kaufland price list was added, and price lists also refresh in the morning, on their own (when Android allows it).
  • More accurate AI matching of the same items across different stores.

Scanning and input

  • Receipt scanning goes through Google’s document scanner (up to 10 pages at once). A note on the screen says: lay the receipt flat, without creases.
  • “Skeniraj” (Scan) on the home screen reads only the barcode, with no pressing at all.
  • A receipt with weighed items (fruit, ham) shows the total amount and the “Cijena po kg” (price per kg).

Guided tour and free packs

  • A 19-step guided tour of the app, as an introduction or later in “Opcije” (Settings).
  • The free version has a bigger pack in the first 30 days (15 receipt scans, 5 dictations, 300 AI links), and a smaller monthly one after that.
  • The savings card is called “Ušteda na tvojim računima” (Savings on your receipts) and clearly says what it counts.
  • The price reports sit right below the Price Intelligence card in the “Cijene” (Prices) tab.

Fixes

  • Pro subscription: Pro no longer switches on just because the Google window opened. It switches on only once Google confirms the payment; when a subscription expires, the limits go back to the free ones (your data stays).
  • The card on the home screen no longer stays on “Povezujem tvoje artikle…” (Linking your items…) after the first receipt.
  • The badge on “Poveži s cjenicima” counts only what you can actually review.
  • The glowing “+” no longer touches the “Moji artikli” (My items) tile.

Coming next

  • Prices and statistics separated (suggestion: statistics go with Receipts).
  • A guided tour adapted to the shopping list and the reports.
  • Sharing a list with a link that opens directly in Nocito.

Price comparisons work with Croatian stores.
