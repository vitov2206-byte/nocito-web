Quick Review

You stay in control

Before anything is saved, Quick Review lets you check the transcript, fix the title, and adjust the date, time, priority, or list — all without opening the keyboard unless you want to.

One important detail: an unconfirmed reminder still fires at the scheduled time. Confirmation is for polish, not permission.

Organization

Built to hold everything, without the clutter

Four default lists get you started — Inbox, Urgent, Personal, Work — and you can add as many custom ones as you like, each with its own color.

Smart default lists

Urgent items can route themselves straight into the Urgent list, recognized from what you said.

Your own lists, your own colors

Create, rename, recolor, or delete custom lists any time. Inbox stays as your permanent catch-all.

Search by typing or by voice

“Find my notes about the laptop” works just as well as typing it — and only ever returns your real, local items.

Recurring reminders

  • Weekdays only, weekends only, every X days

Multiple alerts per item

Set as many warnings as you need for a single appointment.

  • Survives reboots, app updates, and time zone changes

Local desktop bridge

See your list on a bigger screen — still 100% local

When your phone and computer share the same Wi-Fi, you can open a private web view on your desktop — no cloud sync, no account, nothing leaves your network.

Connect with a code

Local network only

Your phone stays the sole owner of the data. The web view only works while both devices are on the same network.

Full backup and export

FAQ

Simple answers before you buy

Nocito Reminder is Android-first, privacy-first, and designed to work with zero internet connection required.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Transcription and AI processing run entirely on your device. It works the same on a plane as it does at home.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no account of any kind.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time purchase. No recurring charges, no Pro tier to upsell you into later.

What languages are supported?

Croatian and English at launch, with more major languages planned before the public release.
